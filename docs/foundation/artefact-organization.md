# Artefact Organization

Once you have created your dashboard artefacts (indicators, scorecards, gauges, map indicators, and reports), you need a way to organize and present them to your users. This is where **Pages** come in.

## What is a Page?

A **Page** is a logical grouping of artefacts that are displayed together on a single web page, accessible via the top navigation bar. Think of each page as a thematic section of your dashboard — for example, you might have a "Demographics" page containing age and sex distribution indicators, and a "Housing" page containing household size and housing quality metrics.

### Key Properties of Pages

- **Artefacts can appear on multiple pages** — An indicator is not locked to a single page. You can assign the same indicator to appear on several different pages depending on its relevance.
- **Permissions apply per page and per artefact** — You can grant or restrict access to individual pages, and even to individual artefacts within those pages.
- **Published vs. Draft** — Pages must be published before they become visible to users in the navigation bar.

## Organization Strategies

There are two primary approaches to organizing your artefacts:

### By Data Source

Organize pages around your data sources. For example, if your dashboard tracks both a **Population Census** and a **Household Listing** survey, you might create separate pages for each data source. This approach is useful when different teams or stakeholders are interested in different exercises.

### By Thematic Area

Organize pages around the topics or themes of your data. For example:

- **Demographics** — Age distribution, sex ratio, dependency ratios
- **Housing** — Household size, housing materials, water sources
- **Education** — Literacy rates, school attendance, highest level completed
- **Employment** — Labor force participation, employment status

This approach is often more intuitive for end users, as they can find related information grouped together regardless of which data source it came from.

## Managing Pages

Pages are managed through the **Management** menu under the **Dashboard Artefacts** section. From there you can:

1. **Create new pages** — Define a name and description for each page.
2. **Assign artefacts** — Add indicators, scorecards, gauges, map indicators, and reports to your pages.
3. **Set permissions** — Control which roles can view each page.
4. **Publish or unpublish** — Toggle page visibility in the navigation bar.
5. **Reorder** — Control the order in which pages appear in the navigation bar.

## Artefact Ordering Within Pages

Within each page, artefacts are displayed in the order they were assigned. You can reorder them through the page's edit interface to ensure the most important information appears first.

> **Tip:** When designing your dashboard layout, consider placing summary cards and key scorecards at the top of each page, followed by detailed indicators and charts. This gives users a quick overview before they dive into the specifics.
